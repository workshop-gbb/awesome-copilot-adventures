# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in Awesome Copilot Adventures respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, patient, and specific.
- Critique ideas and artifacts, not people.
- Respect different learning speeds and communication styles.
- Protect private information and never request credentials in public.
- Credit sources and distinguish verified facts from assumptions.
- Accept correction when documentation or product behavior changes.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Deliberately unsafe instructions, credential exposure, or destructive demonstrations outside a disposable environment.
- Misrepresenting generated output, tests, benchmarks, or official product support.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, discussions, and contributions that violate this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation.

For a confidential report about repository content, use GitHub's in-product **Report content to repository admins** option when it is available. For immediate safety concerns or abuse, follow [GitHub's abuse and spam reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

Do not publish sensitive conduct reports in a public issue or discussion. If neither private route is available, contact [GitHub Support](https://support.github.com/) and ask for a private reporting path to the repository owner.

This policy is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
